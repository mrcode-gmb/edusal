# Nexus Edutech Consult Ltd — Landing Page Content

*Career Service Centre Management Platform for Nigerian Tertiary Institutions*

Notes on differentiation from the reference site are marked in `> [design note]` blocks throughout — read these before building, they explain *why* something was changed, not just *what*.

> [design note] The reference site leads with scale (42 institutions, 186k students) because it's an established player. As a new entrant, leading with unverified big numbers would be both dishonest and easy for a prospect to fact-check and distrust. Instead, this draft leads with a **sharper problem statement + a concrete mechanism claim** (the explainability angle), and reframes traction as a founding-partner invitation rather than fabricated scale. Swap in real numbers the moment you have them.

## 4. Core capabilities (three pillars, rewritten)

**Section heading:** One student record. Three teams working from it.

### Pathways, mapped to what your graduates actually do
Every student follows a pathway built from real outcomes for their department — not a generic career-readiness checklist.
- Holland, Big Five & skills diagnostics
- Department-aligned pathway templates, co-designed with your faculty
- Milestones signed off by a named evaluator — supervisor, counsellor, or assessor

### Counselling that scales without losing the human
An AI assistant answers routine questions from your institution's own documents — and knows exactly when to step aside.
- Grounded only on your handbooks, SIWES calendars, and employer briefs — never the open web
- Every AI answer shows its source; nothing is asserted without a citation
- Automatic handoff to a human counsellor the moment a student needs judgment, not information

### An employer pipeline built on proof, not PDFs
Employers shortlist from skill profiles backed by evidence, with full visibility into what they're getting.
- SIWES & NYSC placement tracking end to end
- Match scores that show their reasoning — department fit, milestone completion, stated preference
- Stipend, location, and housing details up front, before a student applies

> [design note] Kept the three-pillar structure (it's the right information architecture) but rewrote every bullet to lead with the *evidence/explainability* differentiator rather than restating the reference site's generic capability list.
